Your story is good. Your video isn't telling it.

Stock music, stock shots, stock scripts. Your audience has seen it a hundred times, so they scroll right past it.

A salesperson pitches, a crew shoots, an editor you never met cuts it. Somewhere in the middle, your story gets lost.

The Story Behind the Story

Told with care. Not drama.

A

The challenge.

In August 2026 a fire changed Old Town Newhall and displaced several local businesses. The community needed their story told with care, centered on rebuilding and hope, not drama or finger pointing.

B

The approach.

I let people tell their stories in their own words and in their own spaces, with open, unprompted testimony instead of a scripted Q&A. Local businesses and neighbors backed the film, and the community screening was free to attend, funded by sponsorship, not ticket sales.

07 / Questions

Before you ask.

Where are you based and what areas do you serve?

We're based in Valencia and serve the entire Santa Clarita Valley, including Newhall, Saugus, Canyon Country, Stevenson Ranch, and Castaic, plus Los Angeles. Travel within the area is built into your project scope. No hidden fees, no surprises.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Santa Clarita Valley and Los Angeles businesses, family owned companies, nonprofits, churches, agencies, and local brands with a story worth telling. If you have a goal and a story, we're interested in talking, and we're currently booking new clients.

What's the difference between a brand film and a documentary?

A brand film is typically 2 to 5 minutes, built around your company's story or mission, designed for your website, sales conversations, and social. A documentary goes deeper: longer runtime, more characters, more narrative complexity. Both are story driven. The difference is scope and purpose.

Do you offer retainer or ongoing content packages?

Yes. Our Authority Content System is built for businesses that need consistent, high quality video. Monthly retainer structures are available so you always have fresh content without scrambling to book shoots every few weeks.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Brand films typically turn around in 2 to 4 weeks after the shoot, depending on scope. Documentary timelines vary by project. You'll have a clear delivery schedule before production begins, and we stick to it.

How do I get started?

Fill out the form below with a few details about your project. We'll follow up within 24 hours to schedule a discovery call and talk through your goals, timeline, and budget.
