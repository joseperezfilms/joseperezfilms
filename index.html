FAQ

Where are you based and what areas do you serve?

We're based in Valencia and serve the entire Santa Clarita Valley, including Newhall, Saugus, Canyon Country, Stevenson Ranch, and Castaic, plus Los Angeles. Travel within the area is built into your project scope. No hidden fees, no surprises.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Santa Clarita Valley and Los Angeles businesses, family owned companies, nonprofits, churches, agencies, and local brands with a story worth telling. If you have a goal and a story, we're interested in talking, and we're currently booking new clients.

What's the difference between a brand film and a documentary?

A brand film is typically 2–5 minutes, built around your company's story or mission — designed for your website, sales conversations, and social. A documentary goes deeper: longer runtime, more characters, more narrative complexity. Both are story-driven; the difference is scope and purpose.

Do you offer retainer or ongoing content packages?

Yes. Our Authority Content System is built for businesses that need consistent, high-quality video. Monthly retainer structures are available so you always have fresh content without scrambling to book shoots every few weeks.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Brand films typically turn around in 2–4 weeks post-shoot depending on scope. Documentary timelines vary by project. You'll have a clear delivery schedule before production begins — and we stick to it.
